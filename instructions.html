Civil Service Form No. 6

Instructions & requirements

Prepare the supporting documents for the leave type selected. File with your Personnel Officer and follow the latest DepEd Division checklist and applicable Civil Service Commission issuances.

Filing reminder: Submit a complete, signed application with supporting documents. Some leave types require prior notice; emergency cases should be documented as soon as possible.

Vacation Leave

  • File at least five (5) days in advance when possible.
  • Secure travel authority or clearance when travel abroad is involved.
  • Indicate destination and inclusive dates.

Mandatory / Forced Leave

  • Coordinate the schedule with the immediate supervisor and Personnel Section.
  • Indicate dates and ensure required annual leave scheduling is observed.

Sick Leave

  • File immediately upon return to work when filed after illness.
  • Attach a medical certificate when filed in advance or when the illness exceeds five (5) days, subject to applicable rules.
  • For confinement, include hospital or attending physician documentation.

Maternity Leave

  • Attach proof of pregnancy such as ultrasound results or a doctor's certificate.
  • Submit Notice of Allocation of Maternity Leave Credits (CS Form No. 6a), when applicable.
  • Entitlement is generally 105 days under R.A. No. 11210; confirm special circumstances with Personnel.

Paternity Leave

  • Provide the child's birth certificate, medical certificate, or marriage contract as applicable.
  • Coordinate filing with the Personnel Section.

Special Privilege Leave

  • Give at least one (1) week prior notice except in emergencies.
  • Provide destination and dates where required.

Solo Parent Leave

  • Attach a current Solo Parent Identification Card.
  • File at least five (5) days before the intended leave when possible.

Study Leave

  • Attach proof of enrollment, course details, and supporting approval or scholarship documents, as applicable.
  • Coordinate eligibility and service requirements with Personnel.

10-Day VAWC Leave

  • Submit an available Barangay Protection Order (BPO), Temporary/Permanent Protection Order (TPO/PPO), police report, or medical certificate.
  • Handle supporting records as confidential information.

Rehabilitation Privilege

  • Attach medical certificates, clinical summaries, treatment plans, and physician concurrence as required.
  • Personnel may request follow-up medical documentation.

Special Leave Benefits for Women

  • Attach medical certificates, clinical summaries, and attending physician concurrence.
  • Submit supporting records through the Personnel Section.

Special Emergency (Calamity) Leave

  • Identify the calamity and affected residence or area.
  • File in accordance with applicable CSC and Division procedures.

Adoption Leave

  • Provide placement or adoption documents and the child's relevant records, subject to applicable law.

Monetization & terminal leave

  • For monetization, submit the prescribed request and leave-credit certification for review.
  • For terminal leave, file the prescribed application and clearance documents; Personnel will certify final leave credits and service records.

Wellness Leave policy

Read the Civil Service Commission Wellness Leave Policy.

  • Coverage: Permanent, temporary, substitute, coterminous, fixed term, contractual, or casual government employees.
  • Duration and allocation: Up to five (5) days per calendar year, separate and exclusive from the 15 days Vacation Leave, 15 days Sick Leave, and 3 days Special Leave.
  • Usage: Consecutively for up to three (3) days at a time, or on separate non-consecutive days.
  • Filing timeline: File at least five (5) days before availment. For emergencies, file immediately upon return.
  • Confidentiality: Mental health information is strictly confidential under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Do not disclose a diagnosis in the general application.
  • Restrictions: Non-cumulative, non-commutable to cash, and forfeited if unused within the calendar year.
  • Purpose options: Mental health care, physical wellness activities, or a general break from work.

These portal notes summarize common requirements. Personnel should verify current Civil Service Commission and DepEd Division issuances before approving an application.
